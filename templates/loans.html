Loans

Lend your {{ CURRENCY }} to other players at an interest rate you choose, or borrow from them. Interest is a flat percentage of the amount: borrow Ⓜ100 at 10% and you pay back Ⓜ110, whenever you like before the due date. After that a {{ (LOAN_LATE_FEE * 100)|round|int }}% late fee is added (it goes to the lender), and anything still owed is taken from the borrower's cash automatically, including money they get later, until it's paid off. While a loan is overdue the borrower can't buy, lend or create markets, and if it's still unpaid {{ LOAN_GRACE_DAYS }} days after the due date the lender can sell or take over their bets to cover it. You can owe at most the larger of Ⓜ500 or half of your cash and bets minus your debts, adjusted by your credit score (a good score raises the limit, a poor one lowers it), across at most {{ LOAN_MAX_ACTIVE }} loans, and you can't lend while you're borrowing.
