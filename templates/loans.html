Your credit

Your credit score ({{ CREDIT_MIN }}–{{ CREDIT_MAX }}) shows lenders how reliably you pay back. It decides how much you can owe at once, and lenders can refuse borrowers below a score they choose.

  • Pay off a loan on time: +1 for every Ⓜ10 borrowed, up to +15.
  • Miss the due date: {{ CREDIT_LATE }}, and a {{ (LOAN_LATE_FEE * 100)|int }}% late fee is added to what you owe (it goes to the lender). Paying it off later earns a little back.
  • Still unpaid {{ LOAN_DEFAULT_DAYS }} days after the due date: the loan defaults: {{ CREDIT_DEFAULT }}, no borrowing or lending until it's paid off, and a “defaulted” tag on the leaderboard. Paying it off removes the tag and earns some points back.
